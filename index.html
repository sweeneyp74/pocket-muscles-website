Common Questions

Where is my data?

Workouts and routines stay on your devices. With iCloud, they sync privately through Apple CloudKit.

How do I delete data?

Delete workouts and routines in the app, or remove the app. Manage iCloud data in Apple Account → iCloud.

Rest timer alerts

Allow notifications for Pocket Muscles in iOS Settings.

No custom login

Sync uses your Apple Account when iCloud is available.
