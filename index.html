Common Questions

Where is my data? Workouts and routines stay on your devices. With iCloud, they sync privately through Apple CloudKit.
How do I delete data? Delete workouts and routines in the app, or remove the app. Manage iCloud data in Apple Account → iCloud.
Rest timer alerts Allow notifications for Pocket Muscles in iOS Settings.
No custom login Sync uses your Apple Account when iCloud is available.

App Review

No demo login. Core flows work offline; iCloud sync is optional and private to the user’s Apple Account.
